# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing disagreement on ideas and evidence;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes; and
- protecting confidential information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks, threats, stalking, publishing another person's private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual publicly represents the project, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project events.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's [private reporting form](https://github.com/100xopensource/100x-credit-monitoring/security/advisories/new) and begin the report title with `Code of Conduct`. Do not post sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporters' privacy where possible, and take proportionate corrective action. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Maintainers who are involved in a report must recuse themselves from its review.
