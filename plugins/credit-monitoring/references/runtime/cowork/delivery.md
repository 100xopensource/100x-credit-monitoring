# Delivering in Cowork

Finished files reach the analyst as attachments in this conversation. Attach the workbook and the
memo at the top of the borrower's folder, the copies the analyst opens and edits. Never attach a
copy under `.record/`. On the analyst's computer an attachment opens the file itself, and an edit to
a copy the store keeps makes the next run stop.

## Handing over a saved result

To open a selected or unpublished result, attach the workbook and memo paths given for it. They are
the copies at the top of the borrower's folder. When no paths are given, the folder holds another
result: say which one it holds, and attach nothing.

## When you are finished

> "All done. {The one most important thing this month, in a single sentence.} Your financial
> workbook and monitoring memo are saved in the folder where I keep my work for {name}, and here
> they are as files you can open now. {Anything you had to leave out, said plainly, and what would
> fill it in next time.}"

Use “All done” only after both files pass their checks and are attached. If either file is missing or
fails a check, explain the partial result and next step without claiming the review is complete.
