Executive summary · the full analysis lives in the other tabs

Quarterly update: June revenue was $13.2M, essentially in line with the $13.2M budget (-0.5%, -$60K), up 6.1% from May's $12.4M. Quarter to date and year to date, revenue of $73.0M runs 1.5% (+$1.1M) ahead of the $71.9M budget and 4.3% ahead of the prior year. EBITDA missed budget in June at $2.3M against a $2.4M plan (-$86K, -3.6%) -- the third straight monthly miss after three straight beats in the first quarter -- though EBITDA YTD remains $614K (5.3%) ahead of budget at $12.2M, so the year-to-date position is still favorable.

Liquidity: Cash ended June at $36.4M, down 1.1% ($405K) from May on stronger receivables and inventory build, though the trailing three-month average shows cash still building, not burning. Cash is running well above the $8.0M planned in the approved budget, reflecting stronger operating cash generation than the plan assumed. No covenant tests liquidity directly on this facility, and no near-term cash concern is evident.

Collateral: Pledged collateral totals $117.4M at June 2026: cash $36.4M, accounts receivable $18.1M, inventory $9.8M, and equipment/property net $53.1M -- the largest single component. That covers the $58.7M of secured debt 2.0x on an all-assets basis, though only 0.9x on liquid collateral (cash plus receivables) alone, so recovery value leans on the equipment fleet rather than liquid assets. Total pledged components grew 9.6% year over year, tracking overall balance-sheet growth.

Covenants: The one financial covenant, Maximum Total Leverage, passes comfortably at 2.39x against the 5.50x cap (about 57% headroom) and has improved every quarter since the January 2025 close (3.33x, 2.90x, 2.66x, 2.51x, 2.42x, 2.39x), with the model and the borrower's own certificate agreeing to within a few basis points. No other financial covenant exists on this facility.

Outlook: The governing question for this credit is whether June's small EBITDA miss against budget -- the third straight monthly miss after three consecutive beats in the first quarter -- signals a pacing issue in the H1 budget's assumed margin ramp or an emerging deceleration; the year-to-date position (EBITDA 5.3% ahead of plan) still favors the former reading. No open requests to the borrower or unresolved flags are carried from this review, and leverage headroom remains wide enough to absorb a meaningful further slowdown without approaching the 5.50x cap.
