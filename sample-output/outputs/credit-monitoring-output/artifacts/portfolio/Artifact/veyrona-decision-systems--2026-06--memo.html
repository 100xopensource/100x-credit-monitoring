Executive summary · the full analysis lives in the other tabs

Quarterly update: Revenue of $19.9M for June 2026 came in essentially in line with the year-ago month's trajectory, up 8.8% year over year, with year-to-date revenue of $104.9M running 2.9% ahead of prior-year YTD ($101.9M). EBITDA of $3.09M was flat to Budget for the month (-0.1%), while YTD EBITDA of $17.05M sits 7.2% behind Budget's $18.38M -- below the materiality threshold used elsewhere in this review, but the fourth consecutive month running behind plan. Gross margin held flat at 46.0% for the month and 45.8% YTD, so the EBITDA gap to plan is a scale/opex-timing question rather than a margin deterioration. The driver behind revenue's growth remains the mix of implementation, licensed-dataset, advisory and managed-analytics service lines; the monthly package does not break June's mix out by line, so this cannot be attributed to a specific service beyond that.

Liquidity: Cash stood at $10.32M at June 30, 2026, essentially unchanged from the $10.26M level 22 months earlier at the start of the reporting history on file -- there is no cash floor covenant on this facility, so this is a trend observation rather than a covenant reading. The cash on hand is being generated from operations, not borrowed or drawn from the undrawn revolver ($62.7M available, unused throughout). Monthly operating cash generation has run only in the tens of thousands of dollars against $3M-plus of monthly EBITDA, with the balance absorbed by growth in Accounts Receivable ($9.6M higher over the trailing three months); on the current trajectory cash is not building toward the 2030 maturity, and that AR-driven cash conversion gap is worth tracking independent of the covenant story.

Collateral: Collateral is a blanket first-priority lien on substantially all assets of the borrower and its parent, Veyron Holdings, LLC, which also stands as co-Grantor rather than only a guarantor -- accounts, contract rights, deposit accounts, equipment, IP, investment property and proceeds, with no inventory pledged (none exists on this borrower's balance sheet). No total funded-debt or equity figure is available in this box's own sourced context this period; the Funded Debt and Net Debt readings are covered in the Balance sheet review and the covenant detail above. No plan or prior-year comparison for the pledged asset base is on file, since the pinned budget carries no balance-sheet plan for this borrower.

Covenants: The borrower's sole financial covenant, Maximum Net Leverage, passed at 4.90x against a 5.00x ceiling at the June 30, 2026 test date -- compliant, but with only 2.1% headroom (0.10x), the tightest reading in the deal's six-quarter history. That ceiling has been cut three times by amendment since close (6.00x -> 5.75x -> 5.25x -> 5.00x), and headroom has compressed from roughly 13% at close to roughly 2% now even as trailing-twelve-month EBITDA has been broadly stable-to-improving ($33.3M at close, dipping to $30.9M through 2025, recovering to $32.2M by this test date) -- the ratchet, not operating performance, is what has driven the compression. The nearest tight point remains this same test: clearing the next quarter's test with the ceiling unchanged would need Net Debt to fall, or TTM EBITDA to grow, by roughly $5M-equivalent to restore a comparable cushion.

Outlook: No backlog-conversion or order-book figure is reported in the monthly package this period, so the forward read leans on the trend already discussed above. The governing question for this credit is whether the covenant ceiling has more room to fall before EBITDA and Net Debt catch up, given three amendments in fifteen months have each tightened the covenant while granting the borrower a bundled accommodation in return. Two items remain open with the borrower: confirmation of the facility's actual interest rate, which is not stated in any executed document on file, and a re-issued Q1 2026 compliance certificate reflecting the March 2026 restatement (the as-filed certificate is pre-restatement; the verdict does not change either way).
