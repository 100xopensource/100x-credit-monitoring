Absent a change in the amortization schedule, cash is likely to continue declining by a similar monthly amount.

Collateral: Pledged liquid collateral, cash and accounts receivable, totals $29.4M against $100.6M of secured funded debt at June 2026, a 29.3% coverage ratio. Cash fell $1.6M (-14.2%) month over month to $9.7M as scheduled principal amortization stepped up under the First Amendment, while accounts receivable rose $0.8M (+4.4%) to $19.7M tracking revenue growth. No plan or prior-year balance-sheet comparison is on file for these components.

Covenants: The Minimum FCCR breached its 1.2000x floor at June 30, 2026: our reconstruction reads 0.9337x against the certificate's 0.9192x, a 0.0146x gap traced to the Fixed Charges lease-payment sign convention (see Data quality), and our reconstruction governs. Outlook: The governing question over the next test date is whether the borrower obtains new relief before September 30, 2026, or whether continued Fixed Charges growth on the current trend pushes FCCR further below 1.2000x; TTM Covenant EBITDA has grown every month for eleven consecutive months (from $25.7M in August 2025 to $30.1M in June 2026), so the deterioration to date traces to the amortization step-up rather than to operating performance. Open asks to the borrower include confirmation of waiver or amendment status for the September 2026 test date, root-cause confirmation on the twice-repeated TTM measurement error, and confirmation that no further restatements are pending beyond the corrected March 2026 revenue reclassification.
