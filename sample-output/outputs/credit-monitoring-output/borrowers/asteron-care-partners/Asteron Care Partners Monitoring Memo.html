Executive summary · the full analysis lives in the other tabs

Quarterly update: June revenue was $13.2M, 17.4% (about $2.8M) below the $15.9M Approved Stabilization Budget for the month, though up 2.2% from May's $12.9M. Quarter to date (Apr-Jun, since the March 2026 restructuring), revenue of $38.7M runs 17.0% (about $7.9M) below the $46.6M budget over the same span. EBITDA beat budget in June at $1.5M against a $1.3M plan (+$257K, +19.9%), though the quarter-to-date beat is thinner at +7.1% (+$259K) against a $3.6M plan -- lighter opex run-rate is offsetting the top-line shortfall rather than volume recovering to plan.

Liquidity: Cash ended June at $18.8M, $8.8M (88%) above the $10.0M covenant floor, and has risen every month since the March 2026 recapitalization ($18.1M, $18.1M, $18.4M, $18.8M). The increase is funded by operating cash flow ($692K in June) rather than new borrowing -- financing cash flow was a modest net use of $51K, the facility's scheduled amortization payment. No month has shown a cash decline since the recapitalization.

Collateral: Pledged collateral totals $166.4M at June 2026: cash $18.8M, accounts receivable $20.9M, PP&E net $48.6M, and goodwill $78.1M. That covers the $49.7M of secured debt 3.3x on an all-assets basis or 1.8x excluding goodwill, but only 0.8x on liquid collateral (cash plus receivables) alone, so the cushion depends on the illiquid asset base. Goodwill is flat month over month at $78.1M and down 2.4% year over year; total pledged components are essentially flat month over month.

Covenants: Minimum Liquidity is the only actively tested financial covenant this period and passes with room: $18.8M against the $10.0M floor, matching the borrower's own certificate exactly. The legacy 1.2000x FCCR test remains suspended (informational only) through December 31, 2026; the certified informational ratio was 0.3682x at June 2026, below 1.2000x and worsening every quarter since the Q3 2025 suspension (1.43x, 1.05x, 0.67x, 0.37x). The nearest live risk is this spec's modeled FCCR reinstatement at 1.2000x effective January 1, 2027 -- clearing that level from today's informational trend would need a substantial EBITDA recovery or Fixed Charges reduction beyond what June's run-rate shows.

Outlook: The governing question for this credit is whether the post-restructuring operating recovery -- revenue and EBITDA both improving month over month, with the borrower's own site-operations reporting showing a declining weak-site count and lower agency-labor reliance -- closes enough of the gap to the stabilization budget before the FCCR's informational path forces a decision. The borrower owes the lender its FCCR reinstatement-readiness analysis (Stabilization Milestone M4) by September 30, 2026, about two weeks from this review, which should clarify whether reinstatement, a further amendment, or continued suspension follows. Restructuring accounting (the TDR framework, instrument-level modification test, CODI, and Section 382 conclusions) also remains open and could still move successor balance-sheet figures once resolved.
